Problem
We need a program to help manage a business that sells products. The business is a bookshop that works completely online. The program will help manage the stock, availability, and prices of different books. It will also record payments and track the delivery process, so customers feel safe. The goal is to make it easier to buy books in the city of Quito through a program.

Overview:
In the domain of online book sales and local delivery, customers often need a convenient way to find available books, compare prices, and place orders. For a small bookstore in Quito that manages sales and deliveries directly, customers currently need to communicate with the bookstore to ask about available books and arrange their purchases and deliveries. When a customer places an order, the bookstore is also responsible for delivering the books to the customer’s location. As the number of customers and orders increases, having a simple way to manage the purchasing and delivery process becomes increasingly important. This project focuses on providing customers with a digital platform where they can browse the bookstore’s catalog, view book prices, select and purchase books, and track the status and location of their delivery. 

Background
In recent years, book sales have been increasing and evolving. Thanks to the internet—along with changing reading habits—new ways of obtaining our favorite books have emerged, one of which is digital sales. Many people prefer to buy books online because it’s easy to find a wide variety of titles from anywhere in the world, on any device, and because they can have the products delivered directly to their homes. 
Despite the growth of online shopping, local brick-and-mortar bookstores face several limitations, which restrict readers’ access to certain books, new releases, or international editions. On the other hand, large global platforms for selling e-books often lack a literary identity, reader-focused customer service, and delivery times optimized at the local and regional levels.
The creation of this digital bookstore, which offers online sales and home delivery, aims to connect readers with a diverse selection of books through a fast, secure, and personalized shopping experience.
